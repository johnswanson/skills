# Round 1 notes (from the editor, after reading all ten drafts line by line)

Going forward: 02, 03, 04, 05, 08. The other five are mined below.

## Problems that showed up in most drafts

1. **Lines that don't parse.** Examples that must not recur: "I asked for four years if I could stay" (02), "could tell
   the truck, 'My daughter built that one'" (05; a man does not talk to a truck), "And every single page of them was
   signed" (08; means nothing), "rolled up in a swivel chair" (03; curled up? rolled over?), "To plug into your grid when
   it's my time" (01; inside a romance metaphor this reads as a dirty joke), "We're fine" (06; who, about what?).
   Test for every line: say it to a stranger with no context. Do they see one clear picture?
2. **Filler to make a rhyme**: "every tab in blue", "by my coffee mug", "a rancher and me", "my dear".
3. **The hook has no rhyme partner**, so it lands flat when sung. Three fixes were found and all work:
   woke / "handed me a Coke" / "and then he spoke"; permit / "never made me earn it"; permit / "like I hadn't heard it".
4. **Ragged verses.** Several drafts have verses of 6, 7, 8 and 9 lines, or drop rhyme halfway. Pick a verse shape
   (line count, rhyme scheme, line length) and hold it, so the melody can repeat.
5. **All ten styles were the same record**: fingerpicked acoustic, pedal steel, brushed snare, upright bass, about
   100 bpm, G major. Each finalist now gets its own sound (in your instructions).
6. **Length**: most ran 280 to 330 words. Aim for 340 to 400.
